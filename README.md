## Gameplay Instructions

### Controls
- **Arrow Keys / WASD:** Move character
- **Left-Click(mouse):** Shoot projectile
- **Ctrl+S:** Skip opening cutscene
- **X:** Summon void
- **X:** Enter/exit shadow-mode (toggle)
- **ESC:** Pause menu

### How to Play

**Shadow: Child of Prophecy** is a high-difficulty speedrun game where your goal is to complete the game in the shortest time possible. The difficulty settings and progression have been carefully balanced through extensive playtesting by multiple playtesters.

**The Story:**
You are a boy cursed with an eternal shadow, prophesied to either save or destroy the world. To prove yourself as the hero rather than the villain, you must hunt monsters and demons that have overrun the land. Only by collecting enough mana from fallen enemies can you seek redemption and have your curse lifted by the Entity of Light.

**Core Mechanics:**

1. **Combat System**
   - Your projectiles deal 10 damage
   - Shoot enemies to damage and eliminate them
   - Each kill of the DarkSlime grants you mana
   - Watch your health. Each kill of the ManaSucker has a chance to drop ManaSucker's heart that restores some health

3. **Mana System**
   - Collect 100 mana by defeating monsters
   - Your mana bar shows current progress
   - Without 100 mana, you cannot complete the game

4. **The Entity of Light**
   - Present from the start, singing a haunting song you can follow
   - The song uses 2D audio - follow the sound to locate her
   - Once you reach 100 mana, she will guide you to freedom

### Enemies

**Dark Slime**
- Health: 30 HP
- Damage: 4 HP per hit
- Spawn Rate: High
- Drops: Mana (guaranteed on death)

**Mana Sucker**
- Health: 40 HP
- Damage: 0 HP, but drains 8 mana per hit
- Spawn Rate: Lower (varies with game conditions)
- Speed: Slightly faster than player
- Special: Can attach to your shoulder and continuously drain mana until shot off
- Drops: Heart (15-second despawn timer, drop chance varies with conditions)

### Special Abilities

**Shadow Mode (V)**
- Transform into shadow form for increased speed and to hide
- Enemies cannot detect you while active
- Enemies will move toward your last known non-shadow location
- Drains mana slowly while active
- Use strategically to escape danger or navigate quickly

**Summon Void (X)**
- Creates a stationary 10-second void at your current position (void does not follow the player)
- Instantly kills any enemy that enters the void
- No loot drops from void kills
- 10-second cooldown after the void closes
- Best used for emergency crowd control, not farming


### Objective

1. **Survive** the monster-infested world
2. **Slay monsters** to collect Mana
3. **Reach 100 mana** to prove your heroism
4. **Follow the song** of the Entity of Light
5. **Find the Entity** once you have 100 mana
6. **Complete your redemption** and achieve the fastest time possible

Once the Light accepts you:
Your Shadow Curse is lifted
You return to human form
(The ending cutscene plays)

Your final speedrun time will be displayed at the end. Can you prove you're the prophesized hero and beat your best time?

### Themes

This game incorporates two main themes:

### Theme 1: Shadow

**How it's incorporated:**

The "Shadow" theme is deeply woven into every aspect of the game, from narrative to mechanics:

- **Narrative Core:** The protagonist is cursed with an eternal shadow, marked by a prophecy that positions him as either the world's savior or destroyer. The entire story revolves around this shadow curse and the journey to prove his heroism and lift it.

- **Shadow Mode Ability:** Players can press V to transform into shadow form, gaining increased speed while becoming undetectable to enemies. This core mechanic makes the shadow not just a curse, but a tactical advantage that skilled players must master for optimal speedruns.

- **Visual Identity:** The game's aesthetic emphasizes the contrast between darkness and light, with the shadowed protagonist seeking redemption from the luminous Entity of Light. This visual dichotomy reinforces the tension throughout gameplay.

- **Character Identity:** The player character is literally defined by their shadow curse - it's not just a cosmetic element but the driving force behind their quest and abilities.

### Theme 2: Now You See Me, Now You Don't

**How it's incorporated:**

The "Now You See Me, Now You Don't" theme manifests through multiple gameplay systems and narrative elements:

- **Shadow Mode Invisibility:** When activated, the player becomes invisible to enemies, who continue moving toward the player's last known location. This creates strategic opportunities to slip past danger unnoticed - literally becoming unseen.

- **The Entity of Light's Behavior:** The most direct implementation of this theme - when players approach the Entity with insufficient mana (<100), she vanishes and relocates elsewhere on the map. This disappearing act forces players to prove themselves before she reveals her true location.

- **Narrative Parallel:** The protagonist himself exists in a liminal state - prophesied to be either hero or villain, visible to the world yet defined by shadow, seeking to transform from cursed outcast to recognized savior. His entire journey is about changing how he is "seen" by the world.
